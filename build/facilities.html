Our Factory

Built for Scale & Speed

Our 2000 sq ft climate-controlled facility in Shah Alam, Selangor houses over 5+ advanced CNC machining centres, a dedicated quality laboratory, surface treatment bays, and a cleanroom-ready assembly area.

  • Milling: 3-Axis & 4-Axis VMCs, 5-Axis VMCs
  • Turning: CNC Lathes with Live Tooling, Swiss-type Auto Lathes
  • Fabrication: Fiber Lasers, CNC Press Brakes
  • Metrology: CMMs, Optical Profilers
