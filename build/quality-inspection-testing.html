Overview

Capability Details

Quality inspection and functional testing provide a critical verification stage within the manufacturing process. Our inspection workflow covers dimensional verification of manufactured components together with electrical safety and functional testing of completed electro-mechanical assemblies.

Inspection activities include dimensional verification using precision metrology equipment, while electrical testing can include high-potential (Hi-pot), continuity, and ground bond testing. Completed units can then undergo power-on and functional verification against defined design and operational requirements before final release.
