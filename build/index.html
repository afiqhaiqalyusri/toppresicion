Who We Are

Your Trusted Partner in Precision Manufacturing

Top Precision Manufacturing Sdn. Bhd. is a technology-driven manufacturing enterprise based in Bayan Lepas, Pulau Pinang. We specialize in precision CNC machining, sheet metal fabrication, electromechanical and mechanical assembly, contract manufacturing, and customized manufacturing solutions.

Our manufacturing capabilities support customers across industries including semiconductor and industrial automation, providing reliable solutions from precision components through to integrated assemblies.
